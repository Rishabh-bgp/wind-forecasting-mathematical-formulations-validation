About the design. 離 (Lí) is fire: light and clarity. It suits a paper that makes each equation visible and checks it with statistical tests. The red seal (印) carries the initials RA. The taiji and the eight Bagua trigrams stand for balance and change, and the cloud scrolls stand for moving air. The vertical motto 明照四方 means "Brightness illuminates the four quarters".
